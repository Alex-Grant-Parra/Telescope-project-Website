3D Planetarium Controls

  • Drag mouse to rotate the sky
  • Scroll wheel to zoom in/out
  • Click a star/planet for info
  • Right-click magnitude slider for custom values
  • Adjust magnitude filter to show/hide faint stars
  • Set your latitude/longitude for local sky orientation
  • Toggle stars/planets visibility
  • Toggle horizon grid (altitude/azimuth with N/E/S/W)
  • Toggle equatorial grid (HA/DEC: Hour Angle and Declination)
  • Toggle the ecliptic line
  • Reset view to default orientation and zoom
  • Use the Time control to preview the sky at any date/time; the grid labels show Hour Angle (HA).
  • Tip: Focus the Time box and press Arrow Up/Down to adjust with rollover. Move the caret over year/month/day/hour/minute to change that unit. If caret isn't available, use Shift (hour), Ctrl (day), Alt (month), or Ctrl+Shift (year).
