# Privacy Policy  
**Last Updated: 11 April 2026**

ASTRA (“the Service”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

This policy should be read together with the Terms of Service and the Software License.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Email address** – used for account creation, authentication, and communication.
- **Account preferences** – such as light/dark mode and account level.
- **Photos captured through your telescope** – including metadata embedded in the image file.
- **User‑submitted forms** – including account deletion or data requests.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information** – device type, operating system, and technical identifiers.
- **Usage analytics** – collected through internal logs.
- **Location data (used but not stored)** – required for telescope positioning but not retained.

### 1.3 Information from Third‑Party Services
- **Cloudflare network logs** – may include IP addresses and security‑related request data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We process your data for the following purposes:

- **To provide the service** – telescope control, account management, and photo storage.
- **To maintain platform security** – including abuse detection and enforcement of Terms of Service.
- **To improve performance and reliability** – via analytics and diagnostics.
- **To communicate with you** – account updates or support.
- **To moderate content** – photos may be scanned by locally‑run AI models to detect prohibited content.

We do **not** use your photos to train AI models, and we do **not** sell or share your data for advertising.

Captured images may be displayed in anonymised form for educational or demonstration purposes, consistent with the Terms of Service.

---

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing (UK/EU GDPR)
We rely on:

- **Contract** – processing necessary to provide the Remote Telescope System service.
- **Legitimate interests** – security, fraud prevention, analytics, and service improvement.
- **Legal obligations** – retaining information when required by law.

---

## 4. How We Store and Protect Your Data
- Data is stored on servers hosted on an OVHcloud VPS.
- Photos and account data are stored indefinitely unless deleted by the user or the Service is discontinued.
- Industry‑standard security measures are used, including encryption where appropriate.
- AI moderation is performed locally using Ollama models; no photo data is sent to external AI providers.

**Safety note:** All telescope operations are remote; the Service does not grant physical access to the hardware.

---

## 5. Data Retention
- **Account data** – retained while your account is active.
- **Photos** – stored indefinitely unless manually deleted.
- **Logs and analytics** – retained as needed for security and operations.
- **TOS violations** – identifying information may be retained even after account deletion.
- **Service shutdown** – users should download or delete data; retention cannot be guaranteed.

All other data is deleted when your account is deleted.

---

## 6. Sharing Your Information
We only share data when necessary:

- **Service providers** – e.g., Cloudflare for security and performance.
- **Legal compliance** – when required by law.
- **TOS violations** – relevant information may be shared with authorities.

We do **not** share data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

---

## 7. Your Rights
You have the right to:

- Access your data  
- Correct inaccurate information  
- Delete your data  
- Object to certain processing  
- Request data portability  

Some information can be updated via your dashboard. Other requests may be submitted through account deletion forms or support channels.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy
The Service is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age required by law. If such data is discovered, it will be deleted.

---

## 9. Cookies and Tracking
We do **not** use cookies.  
All data is stored in internal systems (e.g., SQLite databases), not browser‑based tracking.

---

## 10. Photo Handling and Moderation
- Photos are stored on our servers.
- You may delete your photos at any time.
- Photos are not used to train AI models.
- AI moderation is performed locally.
- Photos may be shared only when required by law, for TOS violations, or anonymously for educational/demo purposes.

---

## 11. International Users
We operate globally, primarily in the UK and EU. Your data may be processed in either region depending on server location.

---

## 12. Contact Us
For questions or data requests, contact us through the support system in the app or website.
